# 🎓 Real Case Study: IGNOU CLIS Examination Blueprint & Strategy

**Program:** Certificate in Library and Information Science (CLIS)  
**Institution:** Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU)  
**Format:** Objective Type (MCQ / OMR-Based)  
**Papers:** 4 Courses (BLI-011, BLII-012, BLII-013, BLII-014)  
**Marks:** 50 Marks per course (50 MCQs, 2 Hours duration, No negative marking)

---

## 1. Syllabus & Block Mapping

### BLI-011: Libraries: An Introduction (4 Credits)
- **Block 1: Library: Basics and Contexts**
  - Unit 1: Role of Libraries in Society
  - Unit 2: Types of Libraries and their Functions (Public, Academic, Special, National)
  - Unit 3: Functional Units and Operational Aspects
  - Unit 4: Library Staff: Role and Responsibilities
- **Block 2: Library Routines**
  - Unit 5: Document Selection and Acquisition
  - Unit 6: Physical Processing
  - Unit 7: Library Records (Accession Register)
  - Unit 8: Library Maintenance

### BLII-012: Document Processing and Organisation (4 Credits)
- **Block 1: Classification**
  - Unit 1: Basics of Classification
  - Unit 2: Classifying Documents Using DDC (10 Main Classes, Schedules)
- **Block 2: Cataloguing**
  - Unit 3: Basics of Cataloguing
  - Unit 4: Cataloguing Documents Using AACR-2R (Main & Added entries)
- **Block 3: Filing and Shelving**
  - Unit 5: Filing Rules (ALA vs CCC)
  - Unit 6: Shelving and Shelf Rectification

### BLII-013: Information Sources and Library Services (4 Credits)
- **Block 1: Information Sources and Their Use**
  - Unit 1: Categories of Information Sources (Primary, Secondary, Tertiary)
  - Unit 2: Types of Information Sources (Dictionaries, Encyclopedias, Yearbooks, Bibliographies)
- **Block 2: Library Services**
  - Unit 3: Circulation Services (Charging & Discharging systems)
  - Unit 4: Reference Service (Ready vs Long-Range)
  - Unit 5: Awareness Services (CAS & SDI)
  - Unit 6: User Orientation

### BLII-014: ICT in Libraries (4 Credits)
- **Block 1: Digital Literacy** (Computer Basics, Ubuntu, LibreOffice)
- **Block 2: Library Automation** (Housekeeping operations, Koha Open Source ILS, OPAC)
- **Block 3: Internet Tools and Services** (Networks, Search Engines, Web 2.0, Digital Libraries)

## 2. High-Yield Question Archetypes in Past OMR Exams

1. **Ranganathan's Five Laws:**
   - "Weeding out damaged books" $\to$ 5th Law (*A library is a growing organism*).
   - "Open access system" $\to$ 4th Law (*Save the time of the reader*) & 3rd Law (*Every book its reader*).
   - "Library location and operating hours" $\to$ 1st Law (*Books are for use*).
2. **Dewey Decimal Classification:**
   - 000 = Computer Science, Information & Generalities
   - 100 = Philosophy & Psychology
   - 200 = Religion
   - 300 = Social Sciences & Law
   - 400 = Language & Linguistics
   - 500 = Pure Science & Math
   - 600 = Technology & Applied Science (Medicine is 610)
   - 700 = Arts & Recreation
   - 800 = Literature
   - 900 = History & Geography
3. **Information Sources:**
   - Patent, Thesis, Research Report $\to$ **Primary Source**.
   - Encyclopedia, Textbook, Dictionary $\to$ **Secondary Source**.
   - Bibliography of Bibliographies, Directory of Directories $\to$ **Tertiary Source**.
4. **Library Software:**
   - Koha is an open-source Integrated Library Management System (ILS).
   - OPAC stands for Online Public Access Catalogue.
