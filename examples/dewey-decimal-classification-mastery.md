## 3. The 3-Step Decision Algorithm to Classify Any Book

Follow this simple 3-step decision tree during your exam:

```
Step 1: Identify Discipline (NOT just keywords)
  └─ Ask: "In which academic department would this book be taught?"
       ↓
Step 2: Zoom in from Main Class (Hundreds) -> Division (Tens) -> Section (Units)
  └─ e.g., Math (500) -> Calculus (515)
       ↓
Step 3: Attach Standard Subdivisions (if applicable)
  └─ .03 = Dictionary / Encyclopedia
  └─ .07 = Study and teaching / Textbook
  └─ .09 = Historical or geographical treatment
```

## 4. Exam-Style Worked Examples

### Problem 1: Classify *"A History of Modern Medicine"*
- **Step 1 (Discipline):** Medicine belongs to Applied Science / Technology (`600`). Specifically, Medicine is `610`.
- **Step 2 (Base Class):** `610` (Medicine & Health).
- **Step 3 (Standard Subdivision):** "History" is standard subdivision `T1--09`.
- **Result:** **`610.9`** (History of Medicine).

### Problem 2: Classify *"Dictionary of Mathematics"*
- **Step 1 (Discipline):** Pure Science (`500`) -> Mathematics (`510`).
- **Step 2 (Base Class):** `510`.
- **Step 3 (Standard Subdivision):** "Dictionary" is standard subdivision `T1--03`.
- **Result:** **`510.3`** (Dictionary of Mathematics).

### Problem 3: Classify *"Teaching Computer Science in Secondary Schools"*
- **Discipline check:** Primary subject is Computer Science (`004`).
- **Standard Subdivision:** Study & Teaching is `T1--07`.
- **Result:** **`004.07`** (or `372.334` if curriculum-specific).
