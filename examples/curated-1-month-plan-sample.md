**Subject:** Database Management Systems (CS-401)  
**Exam Date:** Exactly 30 Days Remaining  
**Target:** Safe First Class (65%–75% Marks) with 3.0 Hours/Day (`90 Study Hours Total`)

## 📊 Weightage & Triage Breakdown

From analyzing the last 5 years' university question papers:

```
[Full Syllabus: 6 Modules]
  ├── Tier 1 (Must Master Thoroughly):
  │     ├── Module 2: Relational Algebra & SQL Queries (25% Weight)
  │     └── Module 4: Normalization (1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF) (25% Weight)
  │     └── Module 5: Transaction Management & Concurrency Control (20% Weight)
  │     └── SUBTOTAL: 70% of Total Exam Marks (Allocated: 54 Hours)

  │     ├── Module 1: Introduction, ER Modeling & Architecture (15% Weight)
  │     └── Module 3: Storage, Indexing & B-Trees (10% Weight)
  │     └── Module 6: NoSQL & Distributed DBs (5% Weight)
  │     └── SUBTOTAL: 30% of Total Exam Marks (Allocated: 27 Hours)
  │
  └── Tier 3 (Review & Mock Simulator):
        └── 9 Hours for Full Mock Papers + 1-Page Cheat Sheet Review
```

---

## 🎯 What Studying "Tier 1" Means (Deep Mastery):
- Solve every past exam question from 2018–2025 on Normalization (decompositions, finding candidate keys).
- Write and run SQL queries for joins, subqueries, group by, and aggregates.
- Draw complete concurrency lock diagrams (2PL, Timestamp ordering).

---

## 🛡️ What Studying "Tier 2" Means (Safety Net Foundations):
Instead of reading 150 textbook pages on Indexing and NoSQL, you learn **The Core 3**:
1. **Module 1 (ER Modeling):** Learn the 5 ER diagram symbols (Entity, Attribute, Relationship, Weak Entity, Multivalued) and draw 1 standard Hospital or Library schema. (Guarantees 80% marks on any ER question).
2. **Module 3 (Indexing):** Understand the intuitive difference between Primary vs Secondary index and dense vs sparse index in a 5-row table.
3. **Module 6 (NoSQL):** Memorize the CAP Theorem in plain English (Consistency, Availability, Partition Tolerance) and the 4 NoSQL types (Document, Key-Value, Columnar, Graph).

**Outcome:** If the exam asks *"Explain the CAP theorem with examples"* (8 marks) or *"Differentiate between dense and sparse index"* (4 marks), you can write a solid, structured answer that secures 85% of the allocated marks.
