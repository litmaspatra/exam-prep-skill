# PYQ (Previous Year Questions) Search & Intelligence Guide

This guide outlines proven search strategies, query structures, and pattern-mining techniques to uncover past exam papers, university marking schemes, and recurring question archetypes across diverse educational systems.

---

## 1. University & Exam Specific Search Operators

When researching past exam papers (PYQs), use targeted search queries tailored to the institution and exam type.

### A. Indian State & Central Universities (e.g., DU, Mumbai Univ, VTU, Anna Univ, AKTU, SPPU, IGNOU)
Universities often store previous papers in institutional repositories or affiliated college portals.

**Query Formulas:**
- `"[University Name]" "[Course/Degree]" "[Subject Name]" previous year question papers filetype:pdf`
- `"[Subject Name]" question paper "[University Name]" (2024 OR 2023 OR 2022 OR 2021 OR 2020)`
- `"[Course Code]" question paper "[University Name]"`
- `site:[university domain] "[Subject Name]" question paper OR "model question"`
- `"[University Name]" "[Subject Name]" syllabus marking scheme weightage`

*Examples:*
- `"IGNOU" "BLIS" "BLI-222" OR "Information Sources" previous question paper filetype:pdf`
- `"Anna University" "CS8492" OR "Database Management Systems" question paper Nov Dec April May`
- `"Delhi University" "B.Com" "Financial Accounting" past papers 10 years`

### B. International Universities (US, UK, Canada, Australia)
Often referred to as "Past Exams", "Sample Exams", "Exam Archive", or "Midterm/Final with Solutions".

**Query Formulas:**
- `site:.edu "[Course Name or Number]" "final exam" ("solutions" OR "answer key") filetype:pdf`
- `"[Course Name]" exam archive "[University Name]"`
- `"midterm exam" "[Course Number]" "[University Name]" site:.edu`

### C. Competitive & Professional Licensing Exams (GATE, GRE, UPSC, CA, CFA, NCLEX, Bar)
**Query Formulas:**
- `"[Exam Name]" "[Subject/Module]" "chapter-wise weightage" OR "mark distribution"`
- `"[Exam Name]" "[Subject]" PYQ with solutions chapterwise filetype:pdf`
- `"[Exam Name]" most repeated questions "[Topic Name]"`

## 2. Uncovering the "Exam Archetype" & Blueprint

Once past papers or sample papers are identified, do not just read them—**mine them for structural patterns**:

### Pattern Extraction Checklist:
1. **Paper Structure & Choice Strategy:**
   - Are there mandatory sections? (e.g., "Part A: 10 short questions of 2 marks each – Compulsory").
   - Is there internal choice in long-form questions? (e.g., "Attempt either Question 3A or 3B").
   - *Strategic insight:* If Question 3A is always Chapter 3 and Question 3B is always Chapter 4, the student can strategically master Chapter 3 to guarantee full marks while covering Chapter 4 at a basic Tier 2 level.

2. **The "Golden 20%" (Pareto High-Yield Topics):**
   - Which 3–4 topics appear in **every single exam session**?
   - Look for recurring prompts: e.g., "Derive the Navier-Stokes equation", "Classify according to DDC 19th edition", "Differentiate between TCP and UDP", "Explain the Hawley-Smoot tariff".

3. **Question Formulation Stems:**
   - **Direct Definitional / Low Cognitive Load:** "Define...", "List the features of...", "State the laws of..." (Target: Quick recall).
   - **Analytical / Comparative:** "Differentiate between X and Y with examples" (Target: 2-column comparison tables).
   - **Procedural / Algorithmic:** "Solve the following problem using...", "Compute the call number for...", "Trace the execution of..." (Target: Step-by-step templates).
   - **Evaluative / Case-based:** "Critically evaluate...", "Design a solution for..." (Target: Framework-driven answers).

4. **Weightage Heat Map:**
   Assign estimated mark percentages to each syllabus module:
   - **High-Yield Module:** $\ge 25\%$ of total exam marks
   - **Medium-Yield Module:** $10\% - 24\%$ of total exam marks
   - **Low-Yield Module:** $< 10\%$ of total exam marks
